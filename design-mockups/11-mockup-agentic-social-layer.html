Mobbin-informed concept pass

Second brains get a social surface.

This pass treats Honeycomb as a warm discovery layer on top of Obsidian, Readwise, LLM wikis, and saved links. Agents do not expose the vault. They find public-slice overlap, explain why it matters, and turn the overlap into one read-together or meet-together move.

Warm discovery People are adjacent through trusted circles, shared sources, or public slices - not cold profile search.
Sources first The primitive is a source plus highlights, not an interest tag or compatibility score.
One move The agent proposes a single next action: read, save, ask for an intro, or send the opener.
Visible boundary Every social suggestion says what was public, what stayed private, and who can see it.

Functional v1 demo

A working agentic social loop.

This phone is interactive: move through the daily cards, inspect source overlap, save a person, edit the intro, and confirm the social move. The demo saves state locally so the system starts to feel like a small product.
