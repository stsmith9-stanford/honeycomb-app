Two scales, one system.

Inner circle (amber zone): 2–3 trusted vaults you actively blend. This is where the agent has the most context, the strongest privacy guarantees, and the densest overlap. It's where Honeycomb starts and where its daily value lives.

Warm discovery (outer ring): people in your circle's circle who are already thinking about what you're already thinking about. The agent finds them through shared sources and mutual connections — never through cold profile search.

01 · Warm

Discovery through circles, not cold search.

You meet new people because your trusted ones are already adjacent to them.

02 · Source-first

The primitive is a source, not a tag.

"Both highlighted this paragraph" — not "you both like agentic workflows."

03 · One move

Every signal proposes a single next action.

Read, save, ask for an intro, or send the opener. Decide-load minimized.

04 · Visible boundary

Every social move shows what stayed private.

The agent names which slice was visible, which stayed local, and who can see it.
