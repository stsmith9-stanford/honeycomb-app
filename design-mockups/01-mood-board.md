# Honeycomb — Mood Board (Pass 1)

Four visual-direction buckets distilled from a ~400-screen Mobbin sweep across
pair compatibility, triadic group dynamics, knowledge tools, privacy controls,
shared collections, and conversation prompts. Each bucket lists the standout
screens that exemplify that direction and a one-line note on what makes it
work for Honeycomb's specific differentiators (privacy controls, mutual
exchange, legible triadic overlap).

---

## Bucket 1 — Cream Editorial (quiet knowledge, serif-led)

Honeycomb's most natural register. Treats overlap as a *literary* artifact:
serif headlines, generous margins, oversized questions, warm cream
backgrounds, the contents (ideas) as the design. Closest to how a
second-brain reader actually reads. Risks austerity if used alone — pair
with one of the warmer buckets for human texture.

- **Tolan — "Welcome to your notebook"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/16026b99-b050-4935-ae9b-d7a1452db62e)) — cream background, oversized serif/italic hybrid headline. Feels personal, sacred.
- **Tolan — "The Relay"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/d8e3ba51-2e42-4d98-b587-53b051d15380)) — editorial cream page, brown serif body, soft monochrome warmth. Reference for the trifold editorial layout in Mockup B.
- **stoic. — "Do you get discouraged easily?" share card** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/d16bfa7b-1772-4911-84fa-cb5277c4dc9c)) — pure white card, serif question + subtitle, watermark. Magazine-quality prompt card.
- **Matter — font picker (Lyon / Signifier)** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/e81a1f4a-c9d8-4921-950a-0b7ba74bc767)) — premium reader serif typography menu. The typographic ceiling Honeycomb should aim at.
- **Oku — single book's quote cards** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/ebb235b1-f009-438d-851e-26f42a598bcc)) — clean evidence card with page-number provenance + a lightweight "Scrap" reject action. Vault-review primitive.
- **Medium — "Highlighted by Alex Smith"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/d029d138-2f1e-4517-be89-1e6a85390df6)) — per-person attribution above a green-highlight quote. Direct map for "who in the triad highlighted this."
- **Tiimo — "Question of the day" + member answers** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/ee88c59c-59c3-4a88-9878-435eae614904)) — serif question + answer feed showing each member's response with hearts. Group-context prompt template.
- **Clay — Chris Hayes contact with dated notes** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/75e02ee8-8bc1-4187-8571-ef8dfc70e239)) — beautifully restrained dotted-divider entries with timestamps + linked context. Perfect for the Conversations log view.
- **Imprint — Black Swan card** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/3e907d74-48e3-4159-b191-5f9d15335622)) — editorial serif headline over hand-drawn illustration tile.

## Bucket 2 — Warm Data-Rich (things-in-common, peach/cream cards)

The "celebration of overlap" register. Peach/cream backgrounds, soft round
cards, emoji-prefixed pills, dual-avatar stacks, filterable lists. This is
the bucket that knows how to render *people* into a knowledge view without
making it feel clinical. Strong for the Blend hero and the shared-shelf
feed. Risk: tips toward dating-app energy if the percentages and bars get
too prominent.

- **Beli — "Overlapping Bookmarks — bookmarks you have in common"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/7112eebb-8977-4be4-8e71-1aa7c798ced7)) — two avatars stacked top-center, map of overlap, filter chips (City/Cuisine/Price). Closest blueprint for Honeycomb's pair Blend.
- **Peanut — "Things we have in common…"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/348553dc-4c31-4d69-bb98-990f4f5f2946)) — emoji-prefixed tag pills (🍷 Wine time, 👗 Fashion killa) above a mutual-connections avatar cluster. Theme-as-pill done right.
- **Yope — "anyone is missing? add 1 more friend"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/d50e1b8d-d33a-416b-b68c-377c31a96872)) — two stacked profile photos + a third question-mark slot. Literal triadic scaffolding with the empty slot as the CTA.
- **Bump — "amo HQ — 9 friends gathered here"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/fd413f2e-dd6d-48ed-856a-55c404b4a74e)) — avatars floating on a map inside a soft warm halo. Best "group center of mass" visual found.
- **Paired — "What's in Your Heart?" pack cover** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/b560bf75-4d0a-4888-a83c-003c24610bbe)) — pack title in large serif over warm color block with author headshot. Prompt-pack template.
- **Bumble — yellow prompt card "Question 1 of 10"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/7b60766f-1ca6-47b2-8c13-08ccda60b368)) — single big yellow rounded card with "Send question" CTA. Quintessential question card.
- **IRL — "Start a conversation" group prompt browser** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/93a9c3a3-d2ec-4a55-a435-babe7ab7c789)) — tinted pastel cards w/ chat-bubble glyph + category pills. Group-context prompt library.
- **Bears Gratitude — "Say Hi To Someone"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/ba7e1d81-a0e9-48d6-abab-8a5208b9ce2c)) — warm brown card with day number + title + body + "Write!" CTA. Warm action-card template.

## Bucket 3 — Dark Cosmic / Constellation Premium

The "blend reveal as ceremony" register. Deep navy or near-black canvas,
glowing color-tinted nodes, thin barely-visible connecting lines, gradient
auras, percentage-as-revelation typography. Treats three users as celestial
peers — no host, no owner, no ordering. Best for Mockup C's fundamentally
different layout. Risk: tips toward astrology/fortune-cookie if the copy
isn't disciplined.

- **Feeld — "My Constellation"** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/ed14da84-a383-4ac3-b22c-868284bb61c8)) — glowing nodes connected by thin lines on a dark starfield. Partners as equal peers, no host privileging.
- **Tolan — planet-orbit constellation** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/655fc1b9-8f6f-4d48-84e9-428a251ef482)) — living planet bodies with user labels orbiting in shared cosmic space.
- **Tinder Astrology — "You and Amelia" cosmic compatibility** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/0f62a54b-054d-4417-9ac7-9eaab2d03471)) — two overlapping circles header, multi-dimension bar gauges per quality.
- **Co-Star — birth chart wheel** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/8d47e7fa-5bac-425a-bf76-c06060ed961f)) — circular zodiac with thin dashed connection lines on stark white. Premium typography reference.
- **Co-Star — astrology shop card** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/800af2e7-2aa6-4ca1-9669-ef814d895a95)) — black grainy textured card with white serif and natal-chart line.
- **Polywork — "Discover Shuffle" purple full-bleed** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/4b5494f6-6d17-4e7f-8ebd-7fc112b7a624)) — orbital interest dots around a profile photo, themes as pills below.
- **Spotify Blend — two-sphere cover** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/d4b7f81a-6a86-4b34-8a2e-e5d9ec045632)) — two translucent fuzzy spheres overlap; intersection present even without labels.
- **Airbuds Widget — compatibility arch reveal** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/f3df021b-39cc-4cb9-8c58-17a47486ab9c)) — two avatars stacked diagonally in a glowing arch with sparkles. Makes the reveal an event.
- **How We Feel — weekly three-bubble breakdown** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/c2d72b20-1ea5-4358-8500-a0e67975e5b0)) — three overlapping translucent gradient circles. The actual triadic Venn made beautiful — proves the form scales.

## Bucket 4 — Hexagonal / Cellular (literal honeycomb metaphor)

The brand-native register. Hex tiles arranged in clusters; 6-around-1 is
the platonic honeycomb cell. This is Mockup B's hero — the central
hexagon as the 3-way intersection, three satellite hexes as pairwise
zones. Risk: reads as a tutorial app if used everywhere; works best as a
focal anchor with cream-editorial body around it.

- **NYT Spelling Bee — honeycomb tiles** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/cd86b24f-61e6-4dd9-a5d8-acd825948e8c)) — six gray hexes around one amber-yellow center hex. The literal honeycomb interaction.
- **Fitbod — hex-grid loading** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/8219f170-3a9e-45a6-94af-436e0b4777ac)) — tiled photo hexagons forming a breathing honeycomb. Reference for an animated hero state.
- **Runna — hexagonal achievement badges** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/70f51f06-9e88-4f2e-b8dc-41de3b3dc0f9)) — color-coded hex tiles in a grid with varied saturations.
- **Elevate — colorful hex grid** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/37c2bcb0-9e16-4434-a8f9-5dbbb092668f)) — bright multi-color hex tiles, app-grid entry points.
- **Elevate — vertical hex chain** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/2d0b7540-38fb-4c53-af18-7268c563aaa8)) — hex icons threaded down by a spine line. Beautiful skill-tree pattern; could thread shared themes by recency.
- **How We Feel — 3-ring streak rings** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/0d51df84-ed14-4538-9003-3462ad28d3b0)) — three Apple-Watch rings interlocked, multi-colored arcs blending at overlap. Hex's geometric cousin.
- **Insight Timer — amber/gold premium gate** ([Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/screens/c9d719ab-0e01-4112-9921-940cc17b0029)) — warm tan member card with serif italic + sun motif. The amber palette Honeycomb's hex should live in.

## How the buckets map to the four Honeycomb views

| View | Primary bucket | Secondary | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blend (pair) | Cream Editorial | Warm Data-Rich | Editorial serif for the overlap themes; warm dual-avatar lockup for the header. |
| Blend (triad) — Mockup B | Hexagonal + Cream Editorial | Warm Data-Rich | Hex cell is the brand-defining moment; cream editorial scrollbody is where the actual reading happens. |
| Blend (triad) — Mockup C | Dark Cosmic | — | Different visual direction entirely; treats overlap as celestial discovery instead of geometric truth. |
| Vault Review | Cream Editorial | Utility (clinical) | Per-recipient privacy needs the cool utility register; the items themselves are still editorial cards. |
| Shared Shelf | Warm Data-Rich | Cream Editorial | Items + reactions feel scrapbook-warm; titles stay serif. |
| Conversations | Cream Editorial | Warm Data-Rich | Prompt cards are magazine-quality; logged talks are journal cards. |

*Compiled from research conducted via the Mobbin MCP across iOS (primary)
and web. Curation favors screens that directly inspire Honeycomb's three
differentiators and excludes anything that would tip the product toward
dating-app, social-feed, or one-way-consumption framing.*
